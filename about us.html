About Us

Where every dish tells a story.

🍽️

At Kusinang Gamay, we celebrate Filipino cuisine not simply as food, but as a reflection of our culture, memories, and the people who bring it to the table.

From comforting home-cooked classics to flavors passed down through generations, every dish carries a piece of what makes Filipino cooking special.

Our goal is to bring these flavors closer to everyone—one recipe, one story, and one unforgettable bite at a time.

Kusinang Gamay — maliit man ang kusina, malaki ang lasa at kwento.
